Privacy Policy

Pigeon — Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Pigeon is a browser-based security tool for generating passwords, passphrases, PINs, and usernames. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains exactly what we do — and do not — collect.

Data We Do Not Collect

Pigeon does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. This includes:

  • Passwords & generated content — all generation happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • Personal information — we do not ask for or receive your name, email, account, or any identifying information.
  • Usage data & analytics — no analytics tools, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind are used.
  • Cookies — we do not use cookies for tracking. The only value stored locally is your theme preference (light/dark), which never leaves your device.
  • IP addresses & device info — we do not log or store network requests or device identifiers.
  • Third-party data sharing — we do not share, sell, or rent data to any third party because we have no data to share.

How Pigeon Works

Every operation — password generation, strength estimation, passphrase assembly — runs entirely inside your browser using the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues). No network request is made during generation.

The only external requests your browser makes are loading the page itself and the Geist font from Google Fonts. If you prefer full offline use, the Chrome extension version works with no external requests at all.

Local Storage

Pigeon stores a single key in your browser's localStorage:

  • theme — your preferred color scheme (light or dark). This value stays on your device and is never transmitted.

You can clear this at any time through your browser's developer tools or by clearing site data.

Chrome Extension

The Pigeon Chrome extension requests no special permissions and collects no data. Generated passwords exist only in your browser's memory for the duration of the session and are never persisted or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Pigeon does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing to protect.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date. We will never introduce data collection without clear, prominent notice.
